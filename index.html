Methodology

Prices follow geometric Brownian motion, so log-price is Brownian motion with drift ν = μ − σ²/2. A “crash” is the first time the path touches the barrier S₀·(1−c). By the reflection principle:

X_t = ln(S_t/S₀) = νt + σW_t , a = −ln(1−c) P( min X_t ≤ −a , t ≤ T ) = Φ((−a − νT)/σ√T) + e^(−2νa/σ²) · Φ((−a + νT)/σ√T)

Forecasts are lognormal quantiles: S₀·e^(νT + z·σ√T), with T = horizon/252 trading years.

  • Constant σ and μ — no volatility clustering or regime shifts.
  • Continuous paths — no overnight gaps or jumps.
  • GBM has thin tails: real crash odds are materially higher. The fat-tail toggle approximates this; treat all outputs as lower-bound style estimates.
  • Provenance chip in the masthead shows whether prices are ASSUMED or LIVE (and from which source).
